OUR SAFETY PROMISE

Safety Comes First.

Fun is at the heart of BounceRush Arena, but a great experience starts with feeling safe and comfortable. Our team guides every session so players know how to use the equipment, understand the rules and enjoy the activity responsibly.

Before the action begins, every group receives clear instructions and a safety briefing from our arena team.

Ask Our Team
01

Safety Briefing

Understand the activity, equipment and important safety rules before you begin.

02

Proper Equipment

Use the activity equipment correctly and follow the instructions provided.

03

Guided Sessions

Our arena team remains available to guide players and maintain a safe environment.

04

Play Responsibly

Know your comfort level, listen to your body and stop if you feel uncomfortable.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

Come Ready. Play Confidently.

A little preparation helps you enjoy your session comfortably and safely from the very first bounce.

01

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Choose comfortable sportswear that allows you to move freely throughout your session.

02

Choose Suitable Footwear

Wear comfortable sports shoes suitable for active movement inside the arena.

03

Arrive A Little Early

Give yourself enough time for check-in, preparation and the safety briefing before your activity begins.

04

Stay Hydrated

Active sessions can be energetic, so make sure you stay comfortable and hydrated.

BEFORE THE ACTION

Listen To The Briefing.

Our arena team will explain the activity, demonstrate the equipment and go through the important rules before your session starts.

DURING YOUR SESSION

Play Fair. Play Smart.

Follow the rules, respect the people around you and keep the focus on having a safe and enjoyable time.

01

Follow The Rules

Listen to the arena team and follow the activity rules explained during your safety briefing.

Safety starts with listening.
02

Respect Other Players

Play responsibly and give other players enough space to move safely during the activity.

Good sportsmanship matters.
03

Stay In Your Play Area

Remain within the designated activity area and avoid entering restricted or staff-only zones.

Stay aware of your surroundings.
04

Avoid Unsafe Contact

Do not intentionally perform actions that could put yourself or another player at unnecessary risk.

Keep the competition friendly.
05

Stop When Instructed

If the arena team asks everyone to stop, pause immediately and wait for further instructions.

Staff instructions always come first.
06

Know Your Limits

Take a break whenever you need one and let our team know if you feel uncomfortable or unwell.

Your comfort comes first.

Remember

Every activity is supervised according to the arena's operating guidelines. If you are unsure about anything during your session, ask a member of our team before continuing.

PLAY WITH AWARENESS

Know Your Limits.

Everyone experiences activities differently. Listen to your body, stay within your comfort level and speak to our team whenever you need help.

LISTEN TO YOUR BODY

Your Comfort Comes First.

Our activities are designed to be fun and energetic. If you feel tired, uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell at any point, stop playing and let a member of our team know.

Taking a break is always okay.
01

Take A Break When Needed

If you need to catch your breath or recover your energy, pause and rest before continuing.

02

Stop If You Feel Unwell

Do not continue an activity if you feel dizzy, unwell or unusually uncomfortable. Inform our team immediately.

03

Tell Our Team

If you have a concern about the activity, equipment or your comfort, speak to a member of our team before continuing.

04

Choose Your Comfort Level

You should only participate at a level that feels appropriate and comfortable for you.

Important

If you have any concerns about whether an activity is suitable for you, please speak with our team before participating. Follow any specific instructions provided by the arena staff.

PLAY WITH CONFIDENCE

Play Smart.Have Fun.

Follow the briefing, respect the rules, listen to your body and let our team help you enjoy every moment at BounceRush Arena.
